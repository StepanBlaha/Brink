# Brink — Brand Guide

## Name

**Brink** is a quiet notch on the edge of your screen that keeps your Notion pages and tasks one hover away.

- Always written **Brink**, with a capital B. Never "BRINK", "brink app" or "Brink for Notion".
- The code, bundle ID (`cz.stepanblaha.notiondock`) and storage folders keep the old "NotionDock" name so existing pins, settings and the Keychain token survive. Rename these only before the first public release.
- **Notion rule:** "Notion" must never appear in the product name, app icon, logo, domain or social handles (see [Notion's trademark guidelines](https://notion.notion.site/Notion-s-brand-usage-guidelines-How-to-use-Notion-s-brand-in-your-marketing-689045e1761348f887a3c5c80295e00f)). Describe the relationship with a phrase such as "Works with Notion" or "for your Notion pages", and keep that phrase secondary to the Brink name.
- **Before going public:** run a proper trademark search (USPTO, EUIPO, ÚPV CZ) in class 9 (software). A quick web check (2026-09-29) found no Mac app named Brink. *Brink's* is an unrelated security and cash-logistics company.

## Taglines

- Primary: **Your pages, on the edge.**
- Alternatives:
  - "One hover from done."
  - "Notion tasks without opening Notion."
  - "Check it off. Stay in flow."

## Voice

Calm, short, confident, no exclamation marks.

| Principle | Example |
|---|---|
| Say what happened | "Added to Buylist ✓" |
| Don't say | "Awesome! Your task was successfully added!" |
| Errors say what to do | "Not seeing a page? Share it with your integration in Notion." |

## Visual identity

The design is inspired by [Codenotch](https://github.com/vinzdg/codenotch): no code is copied, and the look was implemented from scratch.

- **Surface:** pure black (`#000000`). The notch should read as part of the screen bezel.
- **Text:** white (`#FFFFFF`), secondary `#808080`, tertiary white at 32% opacity.
- **Raised surfaces** (code, callouts, chips): `#1C1C1E`.
- **Accent:** default blue `#0A84FF`, user-selectable from the presets in Settings.
- **Status colors:** success `#00FF88`, danger `#FF453A`.
- **Type:** the system font (SF Pro). Headings are semibold 24/19/16. Body is 14 and captions are 11 medium.
- **Shape:** a black notch with concave, inward-curving flares where it meets the screen edge. Resting pill → hover strip → expanded panel is **one** morphing shape.
- **Motion:**
  - Liquid spring unfold (response 0.62, damping 0.72).
  - Contents spring (0.48, 0.8).
  - List items appear one after another with a 45 ms delay each (max 180 ms).
  - Opening and closing are mirror images.
  - Honor Reduce Motion.

## Wallpaper palette (graphite)

Everything that stands in for "your desktop" (the demo-mode backdrop, App Store screenshots, the website's notch demo, the app icon's screen, og.png) uses one neutral graphite family. No purple, no peach, no sunset gradients. The black notch (with its faint white outline) has to separate from the wallpaper, so the main wallpaper is a mid slate, not near-black.

| Role | Colors | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Graphite** (main wallpaper) | `#4b5058` → `#3a3f46` → `#2b2f35`, gradient at -70° (top left to bottom right) | Demo backdrop, screenshot letterbox, site notch demo |
| Graphite vignette | black at 0% → 30% towards the corners; optional cool sheen `rgba(217, 230, 255, 0.10)` top left | Adds depth without color |
| **Deep graphite** | `#1f2226` → `#2b2f35` → `#16181b` | Dark surfaces behind content (og.png), never directly behind the notch |
| **Light graphite** (contrast alternative) | `#d4d7db` → `#b9bdc3` → `#9da2a9` | Where the black notch must read at a glance (small thumbnails, light-page embeds) |
| Icon screen | `#5b616a` → `#464b53` → `#2e3238` | A step lighter than the wallpaper so the notch reads at 16 px |

The accent blue (`#0A84FF`) stays the only saturated color on any of these.

**Exception:** the interactive notch simulator in the website hero keeps the original dusk wallpaper (`#5c6b9e` → `#9e85a8` → `#eda88f`, 160°) by choice.

## App icon

- `branding/AppIcon.icns`, generated by `branding/make_icon.swift` from code, so it's reproducible.
- It shows a squircle with a graphite "screen" (`#5b616a` → `#464b53` → `#2e3238`, a faint cool sheen top left and a 10% white rim), a black bezel on the right, and the black notch hanging off it with an accent checkbox inside.
- Don't add the Notion logo or any part of it.
- The social card `website/public/assets/og.png` comes from `branding/make_og.swift` (deep graphite, icon, notch). Web copies of the icon and og.png are palette PNGs (256 colors) to stay small.

## "Works with Notion" usage

- You may say "Works with Notion" in plain text.
- Notion's official "Made for Notion" badge may be used only as their guidelines allow: secondary placement, unmodified.
- Never use Notion's logo as the app icon or in screenshots as if it were Brink's.
- Any website or listing must state: *"Brink is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Notion Labs, Inc."*
