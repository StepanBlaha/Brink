### English version (for an international reach)
```
I'm a front-end developer who lives in React and TypeScript. This month I shipped my first native Mac app. 🖤

It's called Brink, and it came from a simple frustration: I was opening Notion 40 times a day just to tick one task and close it again.

Brink is a quiet notch on the edge of your screen:
• hover to see your Notion pages and tasks
• tick them in place, without opening Notion
• capture a task from any app with ⌥⇧Space ("tomorrow 5pm" sets the date)
• a Today view and due reminders

What building it taught me:
→ Going from the web to Swift/AppKit is a different world. Windows, focus and animation, nothing comes for free.
→ The hard part wasn't the UI, it was sync: making every line in the editor map to exactly one Notion block, so nothing gets lost.
→ Details are the product. The "liquid" notch animation alone took dozens of iterations.

It's free and open source (MIT), with no account and no tracking. There's a Windows version too.
👉 brinknotch.site

I'd love feedback, especially from people who use Notion every day 🙏

(Brink is independent and not affiliated with Notion.)

#macos #notion #productivity #opensource #swift #frontend
```
