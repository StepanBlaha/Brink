# Brink marketing playbook

Everything for getting Brink out there, in one place: the strategy, the channels, a 4-week calendar, and ready-to-copy text for every post, story, email and reply.

**Related files:**
- `marketing/LAUNCH.md`: Product Hunt, Show HN and Reddit launch posts, in detail
- `marketing/LISTINGS.md`: directory entries and pull request texts
- `marketing/social/`: story frames, the Reel/TikTok video, carousel slides and square video (see its README)
- `marketing/screenshots/`, `marketing/media/`: screenshots and GIFs

---

## 1. The basics

**One sentence:** Brink is a quiet notch on the edge of your Mac's screen that keeps your Notion pages and tasks one hover away.

**The story** (use it everywhere; people share stories, not feature lists):
> I kept opening Notion 40 times a day just to tick one task and close it again. So I built a notch that does it in one hover.

**Who it's for:**
1. **Notion power users on Mac** who live in to-do lists and buy lists (the main audience).
2. **Mac productivity nerds** who love menu-bar and notch apps (NotchNook, Raycast and Things people).
3. **Developers and open-source fans**, who like native Swift, MIT, and no tracking.
4. **Czech students and freelancers**, your own network, and a local "Czech-made" angle.

**Three message pillars:**
| Pillar | Proof |
|---|---|
| **Fast:** one hover, no app switching | Peek and tick in place, ⌥⇧Space capture, the Today view |
| **Feels like Notion** | Markdown turns into blocks, slash menu, toggles, callouts |
| **Yours:** free, open source, private | MIT, no account, no tracking, token in the Keychain |

**Always:**
- The non-affiliation line: "Brink is an independent app, not affiliated with Notion." At least in bios, descriptions and long posts.
- Be honest about first launch: "Not notarized yet, right-click → Open the first time."
- The link: **brinknotch.site**.
- **Voice:** calm and short. No hype words, no "revolutionary", no exclamation marks.

**Never:**
- Use Notion's logo.
- Say "official", or name Brink "Notion something".
- Buy followers or upvotes.
- Ask for upvotes on Product Hunt or Hacker News. Both penalize it.

## 3. The 4-week plan

### Week 0: prepare (before anything goes public)
- [ ] **3–5 testers** install Brink without your help. Send them the tester email (§8.1), then fix whatever confuses them.
- [ ] **Trademark check** for "Brink" (class 9).
- [ ] **Accounts:** Product Hunt maker account; a Hacker News account (comment a little first); Reddit (read the sub rules); TikTok / IG / X handles. Try for **@brinknotch** everywhere.
- [ ] **Bio text** on every profile (§4.1).
- [ ] **Teaser:** 1 IG story and 1 X post ("building something for Notion people…", with a blurred screenshot).
- [ ] Line up 5–10 friends to **try it and comment honestly** on launch day. Not to upvote.

### Week 1: launch week
| Day | Morning | Afternoon / evening |
|---|---|---|
| **Tue** | 09:01 **Product Hunt** goes live. Post the maker comment. IG story sequence (§5.1). | 14:00–16:00 **Show HN**. X thread (§7.1). LinkedIn post, in Czech (§7.3). Reply to everything. |
| **Wed** | Reply to comments, fix bugs. | **r/macapps** post. TikTok/Reel #1 (§6.1). |
| **Thu** | Ship **0.10.x** with the first fixes. | **r/Notion** (or its weekly promo thread). IG story: "thank you + what's fixed". |
| **Fri** | Directory forms: AlternativeTo, MacUpdate. | Reel #2 (§6.2). |
| **Sat–Sun** | Rest, and reply to comments. | Collect feedback into GitHub issues. |

### Week 2: second wave
- **r/opensource, r/swift, r/SwiftUI:** the technical story, with code snippets of the notch morph animation and the block sync.
- **Awesome-list PRs** (`LISTINGS.md`): one or two a day.
- **Outreach:** 10 Notion creators and newsletters (§8.3).
- **Czech media email** (§8.4).
- **Reel #3** (§6.3), and an IG carousel (§5.3).
- **Indie Hackers:** a "launched my first Mac app, here's week 1" post with real numbers.

### Weeks 3–4: keep it alive
- **Content:** 2 short videos a week. Rotate: feature demos, "Notion setup + Brink", behind the scenes of building it.
- **Product:** a release with a short "what's new" post, e.g. 0.11 (notarized, if you've paid for it).
- **Community:** reply to every GitHub issue within 24 h. Tag easy ones `good first issue`.
- **Newsletter or blog** (optional): "How I built a notch app in X days", which HN and Reddit tend to like.

## 6. Short video (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)
Use the same video on all three. The base is `marketing/social/reel.mp4`, which has no audio: add a **trending sound** in the app, quiet lo-fi or a "satisfying" sound. Hooks matter most. The first 1.5 s decides everything.

### 6.1 Reel #1: "The notch" (launch)
- **0–1.5 s:** a close-up of the notch unfolding. Text: *"POV: you stop opening Notion 40x a day"*
- **1.5–6 s:** hover → peek → tick, with a satisfying check sound.
- **6–12 s:** click → editor → type `[] buy milk` and it becomes a checkbox.
- **12–18 s:** ⌥⇧Space → "call Anna tomorrow 5pm" → the date chip appears.
- **18–22 s:** end card: *"Brink · free · link in bio"*

**Caption:** `This notch replaced opening Notion 40 times a day 🖤 Free + open source, link in bio #notion #macsetup #productivity #desksetup #macapps`

### 6.2 Reel #2: "Notion setup tour"
Screen-record your real (tidy) setup: a Buylist, a Today view and reminders.
- Hook: *"My Notion setup, but it lives on the edge of my screen"*

### 6.3 Reel #3: "I built this"
You on camera, 3 s: *"I built a Mac app because I was annoyed"* (add your age or "as a student" if you like). Then the demo, then *"it's free."*
- Face-to-camera, builder-story videos do very well on TikTok.

### 6.4 More hook ideas, for weeks 3–4
- "3 Mac apps that feel illegal to know about (#1 is free)", with Brink as #1.
- "Stop alt-tabbing to Notion."
- "The MacBook notch finally has a job."
- "Things 3 people won't like this" (friendly rivalry).
- A satisfying ASMR clip: just ticking checkboxes with sound.

**Hashtags (a mix):** #notion #notiontips #macos #macapps #productivity #studytok #studywithme #desksetup #macsetup #indiedev #buildinpublic #opensource

## 9. Replies to common comments
| They say | You reply |
|---|---|
| "Is this official Notion?" | "No, it's an independent app. It uses Notion's public API with your own integration." |
| "Why not just use the Notion app?" | "Totally fair. Brink is for the quick stuff: tick, add, peek, without switching apps. For deep work I still open Notion." |
| "It says unidentified developer" | "Sorry about that, it's not notarized yet. Right-click → Open once and it's fine after that. Notarization is next." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Brink talks only to Notion. The token stays in your Keychain, and there's no server, account or analytics. The code is open, so you can check." |
| "Windows / iPhone version?" | "Mac only for now. Would you use an iPhone quick-capture app? Collecting interest." |
| "Feature X please" | "Great idea, I added it here: [GitHub issue link]. Follow it for updates." |
| A bug report | "Thanks, that's a bug. Tracking it here [link], and a fix should ship this week." Then actually ship it. |
| Harsh criticism | "Fair point, thanks for being direct. I'll look at [specific part]." Stay calm, never argue. |

## 10. What to measure (weekly, in a note or spreadsheet)
- **GitHub:** release downloads (per version), stars, issues opened and closed.
- **Product Hunt:** rank, upvotes, comments.
- **Social:** views and follows on the best video, link-in-bio clicks (IG shows these).
- **Site:** Brink has no analytics by choice. If you want a privacy-friendly count, add Plausible, GoatCounter or Cloudflare Web Analytics later, and mention it on the privacy page.
- **Qualitative:** the 3 most common requests and the 3 most common problems. These drive the next release.

**Realistic goals for month 1:** 300–1,000 downloads, 100+ GitHub stars, 20+ pieces of real feedback, and 1 creator or newsletter mention.
