<p>Brink is a Mac and Windows app that shows your Notion pages and tasks in a notch at the edge of your screen. It is built to keep your data on your computer.</p>
<h2>What Brink stores</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Data</th>
<th>Where it is stored</th>
<th>Why</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Your Notion credential: an OAuth access token and refresh token (Connect to Notion), or a pasted integration token</td>
<td>macOS Keychain (service <code>cz.stepanblaha.notiondock</code>)</td>
<td>To call the Notion API on your behalf</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Workspace name and icon (Connect to Notion only)</td>
<td>macOS Keychain, next to the token</td>
<td>To show which workspace is connected</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Your pins, groups, icons and saved views</td>
<td><code>~/Library/Application Support/NotionDock/</code></td>
<td>To remember what you pinned</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Cached page content, rows, covers and images</td>
<td><code>~/Library/Application Support/NotionDock/cache/</code></td>
<td>So the panel opens instantly</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Unsynced edits</td>
<td><code>~/Library/Application Support/NotionDock/pending.json</code></td>
<td>So edits made offline are not lost</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Settings (accent color, size, shortcuts)</td>
<td>macOS user defaults</td>
<td>To remember your preferences</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<h2>Where your data goes</h2>
<ul>
<li>Brink talks <strong>only to the Notion API</strong> (<code>api.notion.com</code>), to the image and file URLs Notion returns for your own pages, and (only when you use Connect to Notion) to the sign-in relay described below.</li>
<li>It sends your token and your page content there to read and save your pages, under <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.notion.so/notion/Privacy-Policy-3468d120cf614d4c9014c09f6adc9091">Notion&#39;s privacy policy</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Connect to Notion (sign-in broker):</strong> Notion&#39;s sign-in needs a secret that can&#39;t safely ship inside an app, so the one-time code exchange goes through a small relay the developer runs on Cloudflare Workers (<code>brink-auth…workers.dev</code>). The relay passes Notion&#39;s one-time code (or, later, your refresh token) to Notion and returns the new tokens to your Mac. It <strong>stores nothing and logs nothing</strong>: no database, no request logs. It also never sees your pages. Your page content always goes directly between your Mac and <code>api.notion.com</code>. Cloudflare processes the connection under <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/">Cloudflare&#39;s privacy policy</a>. If you paste an integration token instead, the relay is never used.</li>
<li><strong>Apple Notes (optional, Mac only):</strong> if you pin Apple Notes folders or notes, Brink reads and writes them through macOS Automation (Apple Events) with the Notes app on your Mac, after you allow &quot;Brink wants to control Notes&quot;. This access is local only: it touches just the folders and notes you pin, nothing is sent to the developer or any server, and nothing leaves your Mac except through your own iCloud (Notes&#39; own sync to your iPhone). Revoke it in System Settings → Privacy &amp; Security → Automation.</li>
<li><strong>No analytics, tracking, advertising, crash reporting or telemetry.</strong> Apart from that sign-in relay, Brink has no server of its own, and none of your content is sent to its developer.</li>
<li><strong>Diagnostic logs:</strong> Brink writes local diagnostic logs to the macOS unified log (subsystem <code>cz.stepanblaha.notiondock</code>). They can include block IDs and error messages, but never your token. They stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Your control</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Disconnect:</strong> Settings → Connection → Disconnect removes the token, the refresh token and the workspace info from the Keychain.</li>
<li><strong>Choose pages:</strong> with Connect to Notion, you pick the pages Brink can see on Notion&#39;s consent screen. Connect again at any time to change the selection.</li>
<li><strong>Revoke access:</strong> remove the integration in Notion (Settings → Connections), and Brink immediately loses access.</li>
<li><strong>Delete all local data:</strong> quit Brink, delete <code>~/Library/Application Support/NotionDock/</code>, then remove the Keychain items (or use Disconnect first).</li>
</ul>
<h2>Brink for Windows</h2>
<p>The Windows version works the same way and talks only to the same servers. It stores data here instead:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Data</th>
<th>Where it is stored</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Notion credential (access token, refresh token or pasted integration token) and workspace name</td>
<td>Windows Credential Manager, as generic credentials whose target names start with <code>cz.stepanblaha.brink</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Pins, groups, icons, saved views and unsynced edits (<code>pending.json</code>)</td>
<td><code>%APPDATA%\Brink\</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Settings (accent color, size, shortcuts)</td>
<td><code>%APPDATA%\Brink\settings.json</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Cached page content, rows, covers and images</td>
<td><code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\Brink\cache\</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Diagnostic logs (local only, never include your token)</td>
<td><code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\Brink\logs\</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<ul>
<li><strong>Disconnect:</strong> Settings → Connection → Disconnect removes the credentials from Credential Manager.</li>
<li><strong>Delete all local data:</strong> use Disconnect, uninstall Brink (Settings → Apps → Installed apps; tick the option to delete app data), then delete <code>%APPDATA%\Brink\</code> and <code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\Brink\</code> if they remain.</li>
</ul>
